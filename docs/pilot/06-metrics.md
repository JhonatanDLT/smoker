## Leading indicators

- **Reply rate below 10% after 20 messages:** the message or the segment is
  wrong. Change one variable at a time.
- **Calls but no pilots:** the pain is not expensive enough, or the price is
  disconnected from the value.
- **Pilots but no conversion:** the pilot delivered a report but not a habit.
  Add the CI integration and a recurring review.

## Lagging indicators

- Pilot-to-plan conversion (target: ≥50%).
- Time from first contact to signed pilot (target: ≤21 days).
- Revenue per hour of delivery (target: ≥1,500 MXN/h).

## What not to measure

- Impressions, likes or followers.
- Number of features shipped.
- Hours worked.

Only three numbers matter in the first quarter: **messages sent, pilots signed,
revenue collected.**
