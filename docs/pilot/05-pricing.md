# Pricing

## Pilot

| Tier | Scope | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Audit only** | Schema audit and 30-minute review | 3,500 MXN |
| **Pilot** | Two weeks, 3–5 invariants, one staging database | 15,000–25,000 MXN |
| **Pilot + migration** | Adds source and target schema comparison | 35,000–45,000 MXN |

Price inside the range by number of tables, number of invariants and whether the
client needs help writing repairs.

## After the pilot

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Indie** | $29 USD/month | CI, signed reports, 30-day history |
| **Team** | $499 USD/month | SSO, shared configs, report archive, SLA |
| **On-prem** | $12,000–25,000 USD/year | Regulated clients, offline license |

The pilot fee is credited toward the first three months of a monthly plan.

## Rules

- **Never discount below 15,000 MXN.** Cheap pilots attract clients who do not
  value the report and consume the same two weeks.
- **50% upfront.** It filters curiosity from commitment.
- **No hourly billing.** The deliverable is the report, not the hours.
- **No free pilots.** Offer the 3,500 MXN audit instead; it is a real deliverable
  and a natural next step.
- **Annual plans:** 10 months for 12, paid upfront.

## What to say when they ask for a discount

> "El precio cubre dos semanas y un reporte que puedes usar en una auditoría. Si
> el presupuesto no alcanza, empezamos con la auditoría de 3,500 MXN y decides
> después."

## Invoicing

- CFDI 4.0, régimen RESICO (confirm with your accountant).
- Payment by SPEI; include the invoice number in the transfer reference.
- Keep a simple ledger: date, client, amount, invoice, status.
